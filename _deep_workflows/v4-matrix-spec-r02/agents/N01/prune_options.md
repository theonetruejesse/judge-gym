# Prune Options

## Option A

Keep `Gilardi`, `Ziems`, and `Zheng` in the headline matrix, and keep pressure on broad OpenRouter coverage.

### Problem

- `Ziems` still lacks an exact subset lock in the current campaign state.
- current runtime support does not yet justify a broad OpenRouter model-family claim.
- this keeps the paper larger without resolving its weakest points.

## Option B

Prune the headline bundle to `Gilardi` plus `Zheng`, move `Ziems` to an appendix or explicit follow-on, and state that OpenRouter is a provider-surface control in wave 1.

### Benefit

- cleaner and more defensible headline story
- better fit with current runtime support
- more room to keep OpenAI internally rich while still adding Anthropic and OpenRouter controls

## Recommendation

Choose **Option B**.
