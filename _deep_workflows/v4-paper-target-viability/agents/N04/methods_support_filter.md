# N04 Methods Support Filter

## Stureborg et al.

Useful for framing because it already says evaluator inconsistency and bias are real. That makes it a strong ally in the literature review. It is not necessarily the best flagship audit target because its main value to V4 is conceptual support: `judge-gym` can extend that argument by providing a more systematic regime descriptor rather than simply showing another bias phenomenon.

Classification: `useful companion target`, often `framing/support only`.

## Wei et al.

Useful because it foregrounds prompt-template sensitivity in alignment-style evaluation. That is very close to the V4 thesis that evaluator configurations behave like instruments. Still, it is better as a bridge and support case than as the centerpiece. It can help justify prompt/rubric perturbation as a legitimate audit move.

Classification: `framing/support only`, with optional companion-case potential.

## Thakur et al.

This is cleaner than a pure framing paper. It helps establish that judge vulnerability and evaluator fragility are not unique to the political or social-science domains, and it appears reconstructable enough to serve as a real comparator if V4 wants one. The reason it still sits outside the flagship center is thematic fit, not feasibility.

Classification: `clean comparator` or `companion target`.

## Shi et al.

This is the most plausible "methods paper as actual comparator" option because position bias is concrete, legible, and easy to connect to instrument design. If V4 wants a very clean judge-format comparator, `Shi` is more useful than a generic vulnerability paper. But it still reads as a comparator or appendix-strength case, not a flagship audit target.

Classification: `clean comparator` or `support only`, depending on final scope.

## Filter Result

The methods papers should mostly support one of these functions:

- justify the claim that evaluator sensitivity already exists in adjacent literature;
- provide language for prompt sensitivity, order sensitivity, and judge instability;
- optionally supply one narrow comparator case if V4 wants to demonstrate portability beyond content analysis.

`Thakur` is the strongest candidate in this bucket to cross over from "support" to "actual comparator."

They should not displace the direct, externally legible audit targets at the center of the V4 design.
