## Ranked Shortlist

1. `Gilardi et al.`: strongest immediate target.
2. `Ziems et al.`: strongest subset-style target family.
3. `Zheng / MT-Bench` or `Thakur et al.`: best clean comparator options.
4. `Santurkar et al.`: best broadened engine-expansion case if V4 wants a non-content-analysis target.
5. `Törnberg`: worth a dedicated feasibility pass; do not commit without materials confirmation.

## Working Recommendation

The most defensible launch bundle is:

- `Gilardi`;
- `Ziems` subset;
- `Zheng` or `Thakur` depending on which comparator surface is more attractive.

The most ambitious bundle is:

- `Gilardi`;
- `Ziems` subset;
- `Santurkar` if V4 explicitly wants to expand the engine to survey/opinion auditing;
- `Törnberg` if data/prompts can be recovered;
- `Zheng` as the clean comparator.
