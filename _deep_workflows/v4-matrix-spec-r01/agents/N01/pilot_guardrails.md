# Pilot Guardrails

## Carry-Forward Thesis

V4 should preserve the pilot claim that LLM-as-judge behavior is a property of the configured regime, not the model label alone. The paper should stay geometry-first rather than collapsing into agreement-only or accuracy-only rhetoric.

## Primary Levers To Preserve

The strongest V2/V3 carry-forward levers are:

1. `abstention` as a real operating-regime switch
2. `concept framing` as a large mover
3. `model placement` as a first-class pipeline lever when the task actually has multiple LLM roles
4. `grouping / bundle policy` as part of the instrument

## Secondary Levers

- `scale size` still matters, but more as an expression lever than a certainty lever
- `l3` or stronger abstraction views remain worth testing, but they are not headline levers in the current pilot evidence

## V4 Non-Goals Implied By The Pilots

- Do not turn the paper into a general benchmark ranking exercise.
- Do not make broad correctness claims about contested labels.
- Do not let provider expansion erase the matched-contrast design.
- Do not let the paper become a full-factorial sweep just because the engine can support more settings.

## Implication For The Final Matrix

The final matrix should privilege a small set of strong, interpretable, matched perturbations over broad combinatorial coverage:

- one faithful baseline per target
- a narrow perturbation family tied to pilot findings
- a provider panel that tests whether the same regime movement appears outside OpenAI
