# V4 Self-Contained Study Plan

## Recommended Paper Spine

V4 wave 1 should be a **paper-audit study**, not a broad “everything at once” sequel to the pilots.

## Primary Claim

Evaluator outcomes are properties of configured evaluator regimes, and published “LLM as coder/judge” pipelines can move materially under small, defensible regime perturbations.

## Wave-1 Scope

- flagship target: `Gilardi`
- baseline: faithful reconstruction attempt with explicit drift caveats
- perturbations: pick two only
  - recommended:
    - abstention on/off
    - one evidence-presentation or grouping contrast
- provider story:
  - OpenAI baseline
  - optional one-provider robustness slice on a fixed subset

## Near-Second-Wave Scope

- scoped `Ziems` audit after the wave-1 audit lane is stable
- comparator lane only after evidence contracts and prompt surfaces support it cleanly

## Explicit Non-Goals For Wave 1

- full multi-target launch matrix
- full multi-provider headline comparison
- generic evaluator platform rewrite
- proving correctness of contested labels

## Why This Study Shape Works

It preserves the main scientific idea from the pilots and the V4 discussions, but it converts that idea into a tractable paper: one legible external audit target, a controlled perturbation story, and a narrow robustness claim.
